Rules that matter

  • Filter straws do not remove salt or viruses. Boil water for one minute at a rolling boil, or use tablets.
  • Distilled seawater is safe to drink. Seawater straight from the sea never is.
  • Never run a camping stove, barbecue or generator indoors. They give off carbon monoxide.
  • Ordinary solar inverters shut down in a power cut. Never connect a generator to the house wiring without a transfer switch fitted by an electrician.
